Meet the Cast

Get to know the girls of Crimson High — each with their own charm, secrets, and hidden complications.

Aki Anzou

Confident and teasing, Aki is impossible to read. Something dark lingers behind her eyes.

Kana & Kagome Kojima

Opposites in many ways, yet inseparable. The twins are always together.. or are they?

Miko Minami

Kind, gentle, and effortlessly wholesome. But how much of it is real?

Nene Nobuyo

Painfully shy and impossibly adorable. But what is she hiding behind her mask?

Okami Oda

Elegant, distant, and unapproachable, there is something dangerous about Okami. Are you brave enough to find out?

Suzu Shinozaki

A princess without her throne. Why does she seem more vulnerable than anyone else?

FAQ

Is the game free?
Yes! You can play the latest public build on itch.io.

Why should I support you?
I'm creating the game full-time, which allows me to release new content as regularly as I do. However, that can only work as long as I can live off of it.

What do I get on Patreon?
Early access to new updates, custom renders, and more.

What platforms are supported?
PC (Windows/Linux), Mac, Android

Will the game feature NTR/sharing content?
No, the game won't ever have any NTR/sharing content.

How often do you release new updates?
There isn't a set schedule, but for the last 4 years, I've released new updates every 4-6 weeks with only a few exceptions.

About VertigoAVN

Hey there, I'm Vertigo — an indie visual novel developer creating Crimson High. Being a huge fan of visual novels in general and those made with Koikatsu in particular, I decided to give it a try myself and funnel my imagination into creating a fantastic world, filled with cute girls, that doesn't take itself too seriously.
